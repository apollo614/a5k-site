Apollo Chrono

Watch Faces Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers every Apollo Chrono watch face for Wear OS, including the Told Time, Inner Workings, Vitals and Zodiac collections.

The short version

Apollo Chrono watch faces do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What the watch faces show

Some faces display information that your watch already provides: the time and date, battery level, step count, daily step goal and heart rate. Wear OS reads these values from your watch's own sensors and settings. The watch face shows them on your screen and does nothing else with them.

  • Nothing is saved by the watch face.
  • Nothing is sent off your watch. The watch faces contain no code and have no internet access.
  • We have no servers and receive no data from you.

Permissions

The watch faces declare no Android permissions. If your watch asks whether to allow sensor access (for example, to show heart rate), that request comes from Wear OS itself. You can decline it, and the face will simply leave that reading blank.

Health information

Heart rate and step displays are for general interest only. Apollo Chrono watch faces are not medical devices and are not intended to diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Decorative elements, such as the heartbeat line on the Pulse face, are illustrations, not measurements.

Children

The watch faces are suitable for all ages and collect no information from anyone, including children.
